Solutions

Four models, each answering one risk question.

Each model is an Excel workbook designed for an analyst to run and a reviewer to check line by line. All four are prototypes on synthetic data. Each has its own page with the method, a reference example and a validation record.

The four models

PrototypeTreasury and ALM teams

ALM Stress Tester

Where do funding gaps open when customers pay late or funding leaves? Six stress scenarios side by side: the cumulative liquidity position, the survival horizon and the cost of delay.

Method, example and validation record
PrototypeTreasury and market-risk teams

Hedge Sizer

How large is your rate exposure at each tenor, and what would offset it? Bucket sensitivities, the notionals that minimise a stated bucket-DV01 objective, and what remains after rounding.

Method, example and validation record
PrototypeSharia-compliant funds and takaful investment teams

Tail-Risk Screen

What could a Sharia-screened portfolio lose in the bad tail? A normal model set beside a Student-t fit, a Cornish-Fisher adjustment and the data itself.

Method, example and validation record
PrototypeInstitutions with external equity managers

Equity Mandate Monitor

Are your external managers taking the risks you agreed to? Tracking error, concentration, screening under your chosen methodology and tail risk, by mandate.

Why Sadid

Sadid means accurate, on target.

Built for your balance sheet

Fixed Murabaha prices, repricing Ijara rentals and profit-sharing accounts each behave differently under stress. Our prototypes distinguish selected cash-flow behaviours of these contracts, with the assumptions written down. Broader contract-level modelling is in development.

Transparent by design

Our standard for every tool is open formulas and a written method note, so that your auditors and your Sharia board can check every step.

Run by your own team

The tools are Excel workbooks designed for an analyst to run: paste the data, read the built-in checks and take a one-page summary to senior management. Usability testing with real teams has not yet been done.
