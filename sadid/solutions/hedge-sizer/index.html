Hedge Sizer

How large is the rate sensitivity of the repricing gap in each maturity bucket, and which set of swap notionals would leave the smallest residual?

Prototype: the functions below exist and run on synthetic data. The workbook has not been independently validated and is not on sale. What the status labels mean.

Scope

What it does, and what it does not.

  • Converts the repricing gap in each of ten buckets into a signed DV01: the change in modelled present value for a rise of one basis point in the assumed zero rate. It can be negative.
  • Computes the notional at each available tenor that minimises one stated objective: the weighted sum of squared residual bucket DV01, with a small size penalty. It does not minimise total economic risk, scenario loss or hedge cost.
  • Rounds notionals to a tradable lot and recomputes the residual, so the cost of rounding is visible.
  • Shows a DV01 over-hedge indicator against the model's own exposure measure. This is not a finding on hedge eligibility or permissibility.

It reports exposures and hypothetical hedge structures. It does not recommend any transaction, and it does not assess whether an instrument is available, eligible for hedge accounting or permissible. The legal form and Sharia approval of a profit-rate swap are outside its scope.

Inputs

What you enter.

Bucket inputs

For each of ten buckets from 0-3M to 15-20Y: the repricing gap (assets minus liabilities), a zero rate and a weight.

Instruments

Six swap tenors (1, 2, 3, 5, 7 and 10 years), each switched on or off.

Parameters

The rounding lot, a small size penalty that keeps the solution stable, and a tolerance for the exposure check.

Method

How each figure is calculated.

Each bucket gap is treated as one cash flow at the bucket midpoint t, discounted continuously at that bucket's zero rate r:

Gap DV01 = - gap x t x exp(-r x t) x 0.0001

The sensitivity of a hypothetical receive-fixed swap is approximated with annual fixed cash flows at the par rate, under a single curve and with the floating leg taken at par. This is a stylised rate sensitivity, not a valuation of any particular Islamic profit-rate hedging structure. The DV01 of each fixed cash flow is assigned to the bucket that contains its date. This gives a matrix A of swap DV01 per unit of notional, by bucket and tenor. With gap vector g, weights W and penalty p, the notionals N solve:

minimise sum over buckets of w x (g + A N)^2 + p x sum of N^2 N = - (A'WA + p I)^-1 A'W g

The solution is computed in the workbook with MMULT and MINVERSE. A positive notional means receive fixed, a negative one means pay fixed. The result depends on the objective: different weights or a different penalty give different notionals, and none of them is a trading recommendation.

That is not a 94.9% fall in market risk. The remaining exposure sits in the 10-15Y and 15-20Y buckets, beyond the longest swap, and in the two shortest buckets, which annual-pay swaps cannot offset.

Limitations

When not to rely on it.

  • One curve is used for discounting and projection. There is no basis between benchmark rates and no convexity.
  • Matching bucket DV01 is not cash-flow matching. Two structures with similar DV01 can behave differently under large or non-parallel moves.
  • Swaps of one year and longer cannot offset the shortest buckets well.
  • The floating leg's sensitivity to its first fixing is ignored.
  • Hedge accounting, margin, counterparty limits and execution cost are not modelled.
