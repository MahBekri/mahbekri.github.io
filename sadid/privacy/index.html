Privacy notice · updated 19 November 2026

What this site collects, and what it does not.

The short version: the site sets no cookies, has no accounts, takes no payments, and does its calculations in your browser.

Who is responsible

Sadid Risk Analytics is a pre-incorporation venture. No company has been registered yet, so there is no legal entity to name here. This notice describes what the site does today and will be revised when the company is registered. Questions can be sent through the contact form.

The online tools

Everything you type, paste or load into the tools on the Analytics page is processed in your own browser. It is not sent to Sadid or to anyone else, and it is not stored after you close the page. Even so, please do not enter confidential data: these are public demonstrations.

The contact form

The form is not connected yet and cannot send. When it is, it will pass on the topic, your name, subject, message, email address and, if you give it, your telephone number. That information will be used only to reply to you and to handle your enquiry. It will not be sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list. Do not include confidential or client data in a message.

You can ask for your message and details to be deleted at any time by writing through the same form.

What the site does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking pixels.
  • It has no user accounts and takes no payments.
  • It does not accept uploads of portfolios or balance sheets to any server.
