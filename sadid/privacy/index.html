Privacy notice · updated 19 November 2026

What this site collects, and what it does not.

The short version: the site sets no cookies, has no accounts, takes no payments, and does its calculations in your browser.

Who is responsible

Sadid Risk Analytics is a pre-incorporation venture. No company has been registered yet, so there is no company to name here. Until one is registered, the founder of the venture is personally responsible for the site and for any personal data it handles. The contact form is not connected yet, so the only working route for questions and privacy requests today is the LinkedIn link shown beside the contact form. A dedicated address will replace it.

This notice describes what the site does today. It is not yet a complete legal notice: the lawful basis, retention periods, transfers and complaint routes will be set out, for the jurisdictions that apply, before the contact form is switched on.

The online tools

Everything you type, paste or load into the tools on the Analytics page is processed in your own browser. The tools are designed not to transmit or store what you enter. The Liquidity Delay Explorer and the tail-risk calculator were written for this site and contain no code that sends or saves inputs. The return analysis and stable distribution tools are earlier research tools, and their network behaviour has not been separately inspected for this notice. Please do not enter confidential data: these are public demonstrations.

The contact form

The form is not connected yet and cannot send. When it is, it will pass on the topic, your name, subject, message, email address and, if you give it, your telephone number. That information will be used only to reply to you and to handle your enquiry. It will not be sold, shared for marketing or added to a mailing list. Do not include confidential or client data in a message.

Once the form is live you will be able to ask for your message and details to be deleted. Until then no message can be sent, so there is nothing to delete.

What the site does not do

  • It sets no cookies and carries no advertising. The only analytics are the cookieless page-view counts on the two embedded research tools, listed above.
  • It has no user accounts and takes no payments.
  • It does not accept uploads of portfolios or balance sheets to any server.
