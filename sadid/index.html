Solutions

One prototype and three tools on the roadmap, each answering one hard question.

ALM Stress Tester

Planned
Treasury and ALM teams

Where do funding gaps open when customers pay late or deposits leave? The first version will cover liquidity stress: enter your book, run the scenario library, and take a one-page summary to your ALCO. Profit-rate sensitivity will follow.

  • Excel model with scenario library
  • Liquidity gap by time bucket
  • Method note and board summary sheet

Hedge Sizer

In development
Treasury and market-risk teams

How large is your rate exposure at each tenor, and what would offset it? The workbook reads your repricing gaps and computes the swap notionals that minimise residual sensitivity across maturity buckets, then shows what remains after rounding to tradable sizes. It reports exposures and hypothetical hedge structures. It does not recommend trades.

  • Formula-only workbook, no macros
  • Sensitivity before and after the hedge
  • Residual exposure after rounding to lots

Tail-Risk Screen

Planned
Sharia-compliant funds and takaful investment teams

What could your Sharia-screened portfolio lose in the bad tail? VaR computed under a normal distribution can understate extreme losses when returns have heavy tails. This report will measure expected shortfall with models designed for them. Try the online preview.

  • Portfolio tail-risk report
  • Normal-model VaR beside heavy-tailed expected shortfall
  • Model file and method note

Equity Mandate Monitor

Prototype
Institutions with external equity managers

Are your external managers taking the risks you agreed to? One view of every mandate against its benchmark: realised tracking error, concentration, screening under your chosen methodology and tail risk. You update it by refreshing the inputs each quarter. Missing data shows as incomplete, never as a pass.

  • Multi-manager oversight workbook
  • Tracking error and concentration by mandate
  • Screening breaches and missing data flagged
