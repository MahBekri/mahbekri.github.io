Expertise

Quantitative risk and Islamic finance, combined.

Most risk models come from one discipline and are adapted to the other. Sadid builds where the two meet: every model is specified with the mathematics and the contract in view together. Sadid is a founder-led venture in development, and the capabilities below are research and practice expertise, not a list of finished products.

Quantitative risk

  • Tail riskHeavy-tailed and stable distributions, expected shortfall, coherent risk measures
  • Balance-sheet riskALM, liquidity stress testing, behavioural cash-flow modelling
  • Hedging analyticsBucketed rate sensitivity and hedge sizing
  • Portfolio oversightTracking error, concentration and mandate monitoring
  • FoundationsDoctoral training in economics and finance, and in physics. Peer-reviewed research

Islamic finance

  • ContractsMurabaha, Ijara, Sukuk and profit-sharing accounts, and how each behaves under stress
  • Hedging and Sharia reviewMeasuring exposure and hedge size as input to a Sharia review, not as a substitute for one
  • ScreeningSharia screening methodologies and their effect on portfolio risk
  • GhararResearch on contractual uncertainty and its measurement
  • PracticeMore than ten years of research and risk management practice in Islamic finance

Working languages: Arabic, French, English and German.
