Products

Three tools, each answering one hard question.

ALM Stress Tester

Treasury and ALM teams

Where do funding gaps open when customers pay late, profit rates move or deposits leave? Enter your book once, run the scenario library, and take a one-page summary to your ALCO.

  • Excel model with scenario library
  • Liquidity gap by time bucket
  • Method note and board summary sheet

Hedge Sizer

Treasury and market-risk teams

How much should you hedge at each tenor? The workbook reads your repricing gaps and returns the profit-rate swap notionals that bring rate sensitivity close to zero in every maturity bucket.

  • Formula-only workbook, no macros
  • Sensitivity before and after the hedge
  • Tickets rounded to trading lots

Tail-Risk Screen

Sharia-compliant funds and takaful investment teams

What could your Sharia-screened portfolio lose in the bad tail? Standard VaR assumes calm markets. This report measures expected shortfall with models designed for extreme moves.

  • Portfolio tail-risk report
  • Standard VaR beside heavy-tailed expected shortfall
  • Model file and method note

About

Founded by a risk researcher who has worked inside Islamic development finance.

Sadid Risk Analytics was founded by Dr Mahmoud Bekri, an economist and physicist trained at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. His research covers heavy-tailed distributions, coherent risk measures and Islamic finance.
