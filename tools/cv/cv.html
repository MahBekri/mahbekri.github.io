Background

Mahmoud Bekri is a Research Economist at the Islamic Development Bank Institute (IsDBI). He holds a PhD in Economics and Finance and a German Diploma in Physics (equivalent to a bachelor’s and a master’s degree combined), both from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in Germany, where he subsequently worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Economic Theory and Statistics.

With a strong foundation in mathematics and physics, his advanced quantitative and analytical skills deepen his expertise in economics and finance. His career combines academic and professional work: he has taught advanced courses in financial engineering, contributed to research in Islamic, ethical and social finance, and advised international banks and asset management firms on advanced financial methodologies. His earlier roles include strategist, project manager and sales manager. He publishes regularly in peer-reviewed journals.

Since joining the IsDB Group in 2015 through the Young Professionals Programme, he has played a key role in numerous projects and led global initiatives to advance the Islamic finance sector across several departments, including the Islamic Financial Services Department, the Financial Product Development Center, IRTI and, most recently, the IsDB Institute. He earned the Certified Islamic Banker (CIB) designation in 2016, the Leadership Certificate in Islamic Banking and Finance in 2018 and the Certificate in Advanced Project Management in 2024.
