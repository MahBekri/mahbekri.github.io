Profile

Research Economist at the Islamic Development Bank Institute, trained in physics and in finance at KIT, with more than ten years in the IsDB Group. His research covers heavy-tailed distributions, coherent risk measures (CVaR and AVaR under α-stable laws) and Islamic finance, and is applied in risk modelling, Sukuk and product design, and capacity building for central banks and regulators. In 2026 he is seconded to Model Risk in the IsDB Risk Management Department.
